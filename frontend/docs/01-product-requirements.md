# Product Requirements

## 1. Project Goals
- **Positioning:** Establish the company as a serious, premium technology partner for B2B clients, clearly communicating the pivot from EdTech to IT services and SaaS.
- **Scalability:** Build a robust platform capable of supporting current B2B services and scaling to host future SaaS products without a complete rewrite.
- **Perception:** Deliver a premium, modern, and trustworthy digital experience that avoids looking like a generic agency, training site, or an over-animated showcase.

## 2. Target Users
- **Founders / CEOs:** Looking for strategic technology partners to build or scale their product vision.
- **CTOs / Technical Leaders:** Seeking capable technical teams to augment their workforce, modernize legacy systems, or implement complex architectures (e.g., Cloud, AI/ML).
- **Operations Managers:** Looking for business automation, digital transformation, and efficiency solutions.
- **Enterprise Decision Makers:** Evaluating vendors for enterprise-grade software development and integration.

## 3. Value Proposition
"We are a serious technology partner that helps businesses design, build, modernize, automate, and scale software."
- **Storytelling:** Utilizing a "mountain-climbing" metaphor (journey, ascent, milestones) to symbolize overcoming technical challenges and achieving business growth.

## 4. Conversions
- **Primary Conversion:** Project Inquiry / Lead Form Submission.
- **Secondary Conversions:**
  - Book a consultation.
  - Request a proposal.
  - Talk to an expert.
  - View detailed Case Studies.
  - Explore SaaS Products.

## 5. Success Metrics
- **Lead Generation:** Increase in qualified B2B inquiries via the contact form.
- **Engagement:** Increased time spent on Case Studies and Service pages.
- **Performance:** Core Web Vitals in the "good" range (LCP, CLS, INP).
- **SEO:** Top ranking for targeted B2B technology service keywords in the primary jurisdiction.
- **Accessibility:** Zero critical WCAG violations.
