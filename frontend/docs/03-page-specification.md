# Page Specification

## 1. Home (`/`)
- **Purpose:** Tell the company story in one page; establish trust.
- **Target User:** All personas.
- **Goal:** Drive users to Services, Case Studies, or Contact.
- **Sections:** Hero (who/what/why), Trust, Problems We Solve, Services, Capabilities, Products, Case Studies, Industries, Technology, Process, Why Us, Final CTA.
- **Content Needs:** Strong headline, verified trust markers, service summaries, featured case studies.
- **CTA:** "Let's Talk" / "Explore Services".
- **SEO Intent:** Branded search, "Technology Services Company", "B2B Software Agency".

## 2. Services Hub (`/services`) & Detail (`/services/[slug]`)
- **Purpose:** Detail the technical offerings.
- **Target User:** CTOs, Operations Managers.
- **Goal:** Educate on capabilities and drive inquiries.
- **Sections (Detail):** Hero, Problem, Approach, Capabilities, Use Cases, Technology, Process, Case Studies, FAQ, CTA.
- **Content Needs:** Specific capabilities, realistic use cases, linked case studies.
- **CTA:** "Discuss your project".
- **SEO Intent:** Specific service keywords (e.g., "Enterprise Software Development", "AI/ML Agency").

## 3. Solutions Hub (`/solutions`) & Detail (`/solutions/[slug]`)
- **Purpose:** Detail business problem solving.
- **Target User:** Founders, Operations Managers.
- **Goal:** Map business problems to our services.
- **Sections:** Problem, Impact, Solution, Architecture, Implementation, Outcomes, Case Study, CTA.
- **Content Needs:** Business-centric copy, ROI focus, real outcomes.
- **CTA:** "Solve this problem".

## 4. Industries (`/industries`) & Detail (`/industries/[slug]`)
- **Purpose:** Prove domain relevance.
- **Target User:** Niche business leaders.
- **Goal:** Build trust through industry-specific language and proof.
- **Sections:** Industry Challenges, Our Approach, Solutions, Case Studies, CTA.
- **Content Needs:** Evidence of past work in the industry.

## 5. Case Studies Hub (`/case-studies`) & Detail (`/case-studies/[slug]`)
- **Purpose:** Main trust mechanism and social proof.
- **Target User:** All personas in the consideration phase.
- **Goal:** Validate capabilities.
- **Sections (Detail):** Challenge, Requirements, Solution, Technology, Architecture, Implementation, Results, Testimonial, CTA.
- **Content Needs:** Real client data, metrics, verified testimonials.

## 6. Products (`/products`) & Detail (`/products/[slug]`)
- **Purpose:** Showcase SaaS offerings.
- **Sections:** Hero, UI, Problem, Solution, Features, How It Works, Integrations, Security, Use Cases, Pricing/Demo, FAQ, CTA.

## 7. Company Pages (`/about`, `/technology`, `/process`)
- **Purpose:** Answer "who are we, how do we work, and what do we use?"
- **Sections:**
  - About: Mission, vision, values, leadership, team.
  - Technology: Categorized tech stack (only real stack).
  - Process: 8 step methodology.

## 8. Contact (`/contact`)
- **Purpose:** Primary lead capture.
- **Sections:** Form (Name, Email, Company, Phone, Service, Budget, Description), Contact Info.
- **Goal:** High-conversion form submission.
